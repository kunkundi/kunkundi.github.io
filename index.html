连接与设备管理，一目了然。Connect and manage your devices.

在一个窗口发起远程连接、查看近期设备，快速回到常用电脑。Start a remote session and browse recent devices in one window. Reconnect to the computers you use most.

打开浏览器，连接远程桌面。Your remote desktop, in a browser.

当前 Web 客户端连接首页，输入设备 ID 与密码即可发起连接。The current Web connection page. Enter a device ID and password to start a session.

多个远程桌面，同屏掌握。Multiple desktops. One view.

在桌面墙中查看设备预览，通过分组、选择和分页管理连接。View device previews in DeskHub and manage connections with groups, selection, and pagination.

从手机出发，连接你的电脑。 Your computer, within reach.

支持 iOS 与 Android，随时连接和操作远程桌面。 Connect to and control your remote desktop from iOS or Android, wherever you are.

留在终端，也能连接桌面。Your remote desktop, in the terminal.
